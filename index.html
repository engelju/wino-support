Wino – Wine Journal

A notebook for the wine you drink.

Log a bottle in seconds — name, producer, vintage, grape, region, price — rate it out of five, write what you thought of it, and photograph the label. Search later across everything you wrote, your own tasting notes included.

Support

Questions, bug reports and requests are welcome: open an issue. You will need a free GitHub account to post one.

Privacy

Wino collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking, and the app talks to no servers of ours. Your bottles, notes and photos are stored on your device, and synced through your own private iCloud account if you are signed in — which means only you can read them.

Frequently asked

  • Where is my data? On your device, and in your private iCloud if you are signed in to iCloud.
  • How do I get my wines onto a second device? Sign in to the same Apple Account on both. Syncing happens on its own.
  • How do I delete a bottle? Swipe the row to the left in the list, or open it and use Edit.
